I'm passionate about making sure AI goes well for society. I'm currently working as a senior data scientist at the BBC, with a focus on LLM evaluation and deployment. I previously worked in finance and consulting, and from this I've learned to take a pragmatic and outcomes-based approach to my work.

Recent open source contributions include [agentic](https://github.com/UKGovernmentBEIS/inspect_evals/tree/main/src/inspect_evals/theagentcompany) and [multi-turn](https://github.com/UKGovernmentBEIS/inspect_evals/tree/main/src/inspect_evals/makemesay) LLM evaluations in the Inspect framework. A while ago my MSc dissertation was published as a NeurIPS [workshop paper](https://www.climatechange.ai/papers/neurips2022/58).
